How It Works

Discover how RideChain seamlessly connects drivers and passengers, providing a transparent and efficient ride-sharing experience.

1.Search for a Ride

Enter your pickup point and destination. Our decentralized network finds available drivers in real time.

2.Match with a Driver

View the driver’s profile, ETA, and fare breakdown. Confirm to request the ride.

3.Enjoy Your Ride

Hop in and relax. Ratings and transparent pricing ensure quality service without hidden fees.

4.Secure, Instant Payment

Blockchain technology processes payment directly, guaranteeing reliable payouts and peace of mind.
